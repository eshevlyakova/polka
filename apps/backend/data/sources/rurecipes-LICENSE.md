Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/epishchik/RuRecipes-93k

Dataset author: Evgenii Pishchik, 2025. Original recipe pages: RussianFood.com.

This does not relabel the original authors' content as owned by Polka, or grant rights beyond the dataset's terms. Keep original recipe links, the dataset author's attribution and the NC notice. Reassess source permissions before any commercial use.

Changes: deterministic selection, title deduplication, whitespace/HTML normalization, ingredient unit parsing, heuristic categories. Unknown portions, timing and nutrition remain null. No photos imported. Source file SHA-256 and selection counts are in `rurecipes-import-report.json`.
